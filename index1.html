A school with a chapel at its centre and a lawn for everything else

St. James' School & College teaches children from Nursery through Class 12 in English, following the CISCE curriculum. Students sit the ICSE in Class 10 and the ISC in Class 12.

The campus is at Civil Lines Chouraha, Krishna Nagar, Gora Bazar, Raebareli. Records give the founding year as 1988 or 1998, so we show both until the school confirms one.
